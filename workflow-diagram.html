Good to know

New PC

Double-click Setup.bat once. It installs Python and the libraries; Excel must already be there.

The two questions

Steps 4 and 5 are pop-up boxes at the end of the run. They wait while you do the QC, and No is the default.

Applied today

Only the KDC sales export is put into the workbook. Other files are recognised and listed in the checklist.
